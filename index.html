FAQ

What project teams usually ask us.

Where does Majenta typically get involved in a project?

From early digital strategy and information requirements through model production, coordination, 4D planning and structured handover.

Can Majenta work with an existing BIM or information-management setup?

Yes. The role can be advisory, delivery-focused or embedded into an existing client and project-team workflow.

Is 4D only useful once the programme is fixed?

No. Its value is often highest while teams are still testing sequence, communicating build logic and resolving dependencies.
